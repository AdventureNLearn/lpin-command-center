## Three nested completeness layers

Smoke UI must show three numbers. They nest. They are not interchangeable.

```
196 listed desks          ← atlas.json (every country/place row)
 └── 33 kits on disk      ← kit dir + packages/{iso}.json + {iso}-permit.json
      └── 12 ingest audits ← packages/{iso}-fill.json
```

| Layer | What it counts | What it does not count | Disk set |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **196 atlas** | Listed legislatures / places with `iso2` + name + UN flag + kit flag | Presence of members, votes, or ingest leftovers | all atlas rows |
| **33 packages** | A kit folder exists and was packaged | Roster fullness; leftover-URL audit | the 33 true iso2 |
| **12 fills** | Ingest-drop record: leftover source URLs left as holes, kits not rewritten | “This country is finished” | br, ca, cn, de, fr, gb, it, kr, pl, tw, ua, us |

12 is a subset of 33. 33 is a subset of 196.
