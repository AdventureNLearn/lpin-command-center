## What this is

The **Civic Command Center** is the public civic toolkit. It is LPIN Civic Intelligence **on** the GEV globe, not a third homepage and not a silent merge of git trees.

| Layer | Authority | Public label |
|-------|-----------|--------------|
| Why it exists | LPIN integrity kernel | Civic Intelligence |
| How it looks | GEV / gevradio UI | Command Center (globe, glass cards, drawer, radio) |
| How a tool joins | Workdesk Generator pack | that tool’s **system label** |
| Who decides a claim | Human | Human final call |

Every LPIN product after this sitting **inherits** `09-LPIN-BASELINE.md`. A product may skip the globe. It may not skip the kernel, the labels, or honest incomplete.

## What this is not

- Not a 15-agent product. Seats are **ops contracts**. Public UI never names seats, desks, or skill brands (D-266 / B2).
- Not a source-tree merge. `products/LPINsuite_v2`, Tutor trees, and this GEV tree stay separate. Composition is **packs + labels**.
- Not an iframe wall of grok.me sites.
- Not legal advice, not a government site, not a score of persons or towns.
- Not a ship this sitting.
