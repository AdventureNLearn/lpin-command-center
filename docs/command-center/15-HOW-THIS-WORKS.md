# How this board works

**Lily Pad Intelligence Network** (LPIN).  
**Powered by GOYNET** — Governance, Operations, and Yields.  
Created with the AOS Hive-Brain.

This is a board for honest questions. It does not decide for you.

## How to use it

1. Name the question.
2. Open the desk that matches that kind of question.
3. Write what you found. If you quote a rule or a record, paste the official link.
4. A blank box means we do not know yet. Leave it blank.
5. If the same fact shows up on two desks, that is a lead — not proof.
6. You decide.

## The three doors

| Door | Ask |
|------|-----|
| Governance | Who may act, and what is actually written? |
| Operations | What is happening on the ground, and what was built? |
| Yields | Where did the money and the permissions go? |

Each desk has a short how-to on the board. Follow the steps. Copy them. Teach them. That is the framework.

## How to copy the framework

- One globe. One file drawer. One card. No second homepage.
- Every answer names what kind of desk it came from.
- A quote without a link is not proven.
- Empty is honest. A finished-looking blank is not.
- The same fact in two places is a lead. A person still makes the call.

## What this is not

Not legal advice. Not a government site. Not a score of people or towns. Not a secret feed.
